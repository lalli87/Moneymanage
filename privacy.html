MoneyBook — Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

MoneyBook is a personal budgeting app. It is built so that your financial information stays under your control.

What MoneyBook stores, and where

  • On your device: the transactions, accounts, categories, budgets and settings you enter are stored in the app's private storage on your phone or browser.
  • In your own Google Drive (optional): if you turn on sync, a copy is saved in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive that only MoneyBook can read. Weekly backups are saved there too. You can encrypt this copy with a passphrase only you know.
  • Nothing is stored on our servers. MoneyBook has no servers and the developer cannot see your data.

Google sign-in and permissions

  • Google Drive (app data folder only): to save and sync your MoneyBook data. MoneyBook cannot see your other Drive files.
  • Google Calendar events (optional): only if you ask MoneyBook to add payment reminders to your calendar.
  • Camera and photos: only when you scan or pick a receipt. The text is read on your device; the picture is not kept or uploaded.
  • Notifications: reminders for payments and bills.

What we don't do

MoneyBook does not collect analytics, show ads, sell or share data, or track you. Receipt text recognition runs on your device (Google ML Kit).

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove local data. To remove the Drive copy, open Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps › MoneyBook › Delete hidden app data, and remove MoneyBook's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
